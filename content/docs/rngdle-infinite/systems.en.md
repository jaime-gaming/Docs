Six systems hold up the progression. The first three are used from minute one; the last three are resets that unlock as you advance.

## Companions

**Thirteen friends** that multiply the EP you earn.

- You carry **one at a time**.
- Some are **found for free** while you roll: you do not have to buy them all.
- Switching companion is instant and loses no progress.

> [!WARNING]
> **A companion does not make your rolls luckier.** It multiplies the EP you earn, but your number and its badges stay exactly the same. It is an earnings multiplier, not a probability one.

## Skills

Effects that **charge up** and **fire on your next roll**. Among them:

- **Double EP** on the roll where they trigger.
- **Extra rolls**.
- **No cooldown**.

Two important rules:

1. **Skills never change how a number scores.** They affect what you earn or how long you wait, not which badges your number has.
2. **They only charge while you are online.** Offline rolls pay their EP and stay in the history, but they do not charge skills.

## Tasks and badges

Two parallel collections:

| Collection | What it is | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Badges** | Every distinct pattern that has appeared in any of your numbers | Filling the collection, which is a prestige requirement |
| **Tasks** | Concrete goals to chase | Giving the save direction between resets |

Completing **half the collection** is one of the two prestige requirements.

## Rebirth

**Start over with a permanent bonus.**

It is the first reset available and the cheapest one: you lose the cycle's progress in exchange for a multiplier you keep forever. It is how you unstick the game when the cycle's upgrades stop paying off.

## Prestige

**A bigger reset.** Two conditions have to be met at the same time:

1. Having **half the badge collection**.
2. Having earned **30 million EP in the cycle**.

Prestige resets more things than rebirth and gives a bigger bonus in return. It is the central step of the long progression.

## Rollback

It appears **after three prestiges**, and it **can be repeated**.

It is the most advanced system in the game and the one that closes the long cycle: once unlocked, it becomes part of the reset routine instead of a one-off goal.

## How they fit together

```text
roll ──► EP ──► upgrades (speed)          ┐
              ├─ companions (multiplier)  ├── current cycle
              └─ skills (next roll)       ┘
                        │
               badges + tasks ──► collection
                        │
              half the collection + 30M EP from the cycle
                        │
                  ┌─────┴─────┐
              rebirth     prestige ──► ×3 ──► rollback (repeatable)
```

The practical reading: **roll speed and companions pay for themselves inside the cycle**, while **the badge collection is what actually moves the save forward long term**, because it is the requirement that opens the bigger resets.
